Doctor of Chiropractic

Dr. Eva Zingone

Dr. Eva holds some of the most specialized credentials in family chiropractic. She is certified by the Academy Council of Chiropractic Pediatrics (CACCP), is Webster Technique certified for prenatal care, is an International Board Certified Lactation Consultant, and trained as a birth doula through DONA International.

That combination means a mom can bring questions about her pregnancy, her recovery, her feeding journey and her baby’s comfort to one person who understands all four. Patients regularly tell us Dr. Eva takes the time to understand how they feel at each visit, and that nothing ever feels rushed.

  • CACCP Certified
  • Webster Technique Certified
  • IBCLC Lactation Consultant
  • DONA Trained Birth Doula
  • Perinatal Certified

Doctor of Chiropractic

Dr. Joe Zingone

Dr. Joe is Webster Technique certified, holds perinatal certifications covering pre- and post-pregnancy care, and is an International Board Certified Lactation Consultant — a rare credential for a chiropractor and an enormous help to new parents working through feeding challenges.

Families describe his approach as thorough and genuinely attentive: a careful interview and exam first, then clear feedback on simple things you can do to improve your overall health. His kindness with kids and adults alike is the thing long-term patients mention most.

  • Perinatal Certified
  • IBCLC Lactation Consultant

How we adjust

Techniques our doctors are trained in

We combine techniques to create a customized care experience, unlike some offices that rely on a single technique.

Diversified Technique

The classic hands-on adjustment, delivered with precision where it is the right fit for your body.

Drop Table

A table-assisted approach that lets us make specific corrections with very little force.

Activator®

An instrument-delivered adjustment for patients who prefer no popping or cracking at all.

Sacral Occipital Technique (SOT®)

A system-based approach that looks at the relationship between the pelvis and the head.

Light-touch infant care

For babies, an adjustment is often nothing more than gentle, specific contact — safe and comfortable.

Digital posture assessment

For adults, older children and pregnant women, so postural stress is tracked before and during care.

Complimentary consultations

Talk to a doctor before you commit to anything

We offer complimentary consultations in the office or via phone and Zoom. If we determine chiropractic isn’t right for you, we’ll tell you who we think can help you.
