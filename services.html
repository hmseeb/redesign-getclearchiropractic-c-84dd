Service 01

Chiropractic Care

Our chiropractors are trained in various techniques and tailor their approach to each patient’s unique needs. Our team uses Diversified Technique, Drop Table, Activator®, Sacral Occipital Technique (SOT®), and others. This flexibility allows us to combine techniques to create a customized care experience, unlike offices that rely on a single technique.

Many patients appreciate that we go beyond the traditional “popping and cracking” style. We also provide specific, gentle corrections with tools like the Activator, or even light-touch adjustments for infants.

  • Diversified Technique and Drop Table adjusting
  • Activator® instrument-assisted corrections
  • Sacral Occipital Technique (SOT®)
  • Light-touch work appropriate for newborns
  • Digital posture assessments for adults, older children and pregnant women

Service 02

Pregnancy Chiropractic

All of our doctors are Webster Technique Certified and skilled in supporting pregnant women. Dr. Eva and Dr. Joe additionally hold perinatal certifications, which adds pre- and post-pregnancy care expertise to your team.

Pregnancy changes posture, load and balance week by week. We use digital posture assessments to monitor postural stress before and during care, so your plan adapts as your body does — right through to delivery.

  • Webster Technique certified doctors
  • Perinatal certifications held by Dr. Eva and Dr. Joe
  • Dr. Eva is a DONA International trained birth doula
  • Online community with pregnancy and birth prep resources

Service 03

Postpartum Care

If you’ve recently had a baby, you likely want to recover from your birth and get your life back on track. But with a young baby to care for, how do you balance it all? That’s where we come in.

We want the best for you and your baby, just like you do. We’ll walk beside you as you take steps to invest in your health and well-being — recovery care for you, gentle care for your newborn, and feeding support from International Board Certified Lactation Consultants, all in one place.

  • Recovery-focused adjusting for the fourth trimester
  • IBCLC breastfeeding support alongside your care
  • Bring the baby — families get care together
  • Family plans and discounts for family members
Service 04

Pediatric Chiropractic

There’s nothing better than seeing an entire family in our reception area, ready to get care together. We have a particular focus on prenatal, postpartum and pediatric chiropractic.

Dr. Eva is certified by the Academy Council of Chiropractic Pediatrics (CACCP), and our doctors are known for being kind and gentle with little ones. For infants, an adjustment is often nothing more than specific, light touch — no popping or cracking. Chiropractic also helps relieve the ongoing postural stress kids experience from “tech neck” and heavy backpacks, reducing those stresses early on while they grow.

  • CACCP certified pediatric care
  • Light-touch adjustments for newborns and infants
  • Posture assessments for older children
  • Visits designed to be quick and convenient for busy families

Service 05 · Technology

INSiGHT Scans

Our approach focuses on supporting the body’s natural ability to heal, which means we need to see what the nervous system is actually doing. INSiGHT scans give us objective readings of where your system is holding stress and how well it is adapting.

That means care decisions are based on data rather than guesswork — and when we re-scan, you can see your progress for yourself. We use scans for adults and children alike as part of our comprehensive initial assessment.

Conditions

Concerns families bring to us

Our approach focuses on overall wellness and supporting the body’s natural ability to heal. Rather than treating specific conditions, we look for imbalances in the nervous system. Chiropractic care helps address issues stemming from those imbalances — such as poor sleep, headaches and chronic body aches. For children, this may include colic, reflux, constipation, and even sensory challenges or postural issues. Other concerns, like asthma, anxiety and digestive problems, may also improve with chiropractic care.

ADHD

Support for focus and regulation by addressing nervous system imbalance rather than chasing symptoms.

Anxiety

Care aimed at helping an over-stressed nervous system shift out of constant fight-or-flight.

Autism

Gentle, individualized care for kids on the spectrum, at a pace each child is comfortable with.

Breastfeeding challenges

Chiropractic plus IBCLC lactation expertise from Dr. Eva and Dr. Joe in a single visit.

Colic

Light-touch infant care for unsettled babies who struggle to calm and stay comfortable.

Constipation

Addressing nerve supply and tension patterns that affect digestion and regularity in kids and adults.

Chronic fatigue

Helping the body adapt and recover so energy isn’t spent fighting unresolved stress.

Ear infections

A common reason families bring little ones to see us for ongoing, recurring discomfort.

Headaches

One of the most frequent adult complaints we see, often tied to postural and “tech neck” stress.

Sensory processing disorder

Patient, child-led care for kids who are easily overwhelmed by sensory input.

Sleep challenges

Poor sleep is one of the clearest signs of nervous system imbalance — for babies and grown-ups.

Torticollis

Gentle care for infants with a strong head-turning preference and restricted neck movement.
