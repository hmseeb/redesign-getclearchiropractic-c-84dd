Frequently asked questions

Answers before your first visit

What techniques do your chiropractors use?

Our chiropractors are trained in various techniques and tailor their approach to each patient’s unique needs. Our team uses Diversified Technique, Drop Table, Activator®, Sacral Occipital Technique (SOT®), and others. This flexibility allows us to combine techniques to create a customized care experience, unlike some offices that rely on a single technique.

Many patients appreciate that we go beyond the traditional “popping and cracking” style; our chiropractors also provide specific, gentle corrections with tools like the Activator or even light touch adjustments for infants. We adjust our methods based on each patient’s needs, ensuring comfort and the best outcomes.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment options. At Clear Chiropractic, you can pay per visit or opt for a customized plan with affordable monthly payments, generally between $250 and $450. The plan’s length and cost depend on your personal goals and challenges. We also offer family plans and additional discounts for family members.

What is the cost of a session?

A drop-in chiropractic session costs up to $75. Most patients, however, choose a customized care plan, which averages between $250 and $450 monthly, depending on individual needs, goals, and assessments.

Can chiropractic care help with posture correction?

Absolutely! We offer digital posture assessments for adults, older children, and pregnant women to monitor postural stress before and during care. Chiropractic helps relieve the ongoing postural stress our bodies experience due to factors like “tech neck” and heavy backpacks. Our care supports healthy development by reducing these stresses early on for growing children. It’s a fantastic tool for improving posture and overall well-being.

What certifications do your chiropractors have?

Our chiropractors have special certifications to provide comprehensive care for all ages. All our doctors are Webster Technique Certified and skilled in supporting pregnant women. Additionally, Dr. Eva and Dr. Joe hold perinatal certifications, adding pre- and post-pregnancy care expertise. They are also International Board Certified Lactation Consultants, uniquely qualified to support new moms with breastfeeding.

Dr. Eva is certified by the Academy Council of Chiropractic Pediatrics (CACCP) and trained as a birth doula by DONA International. Our practice also includes an online community with pregnancy, birth prep, and early parenthood resources.

What conditions do chiropractors treat?

Our approach focuses on overall wellness and supporting the body’s natural ability to heal. Rather than treating specific conditions, we look for imbalances in the nervous system. Chiropractic care helps address issues stemming from these imbalances, such as poor sleep, headaches, and chronic body aches.

For children, this may include colic, reflux, constipation, and even sensory challenges or postural issues. Other conditions, like asthma, anxiety, and digestive problems, may also improve with chiropractic care.

How long does each session last?

Thanks to our comprehensive initial assessment, typical adjustments are focused and efficient, usually lasting around 5–10 minutes. We understand that many families have busy schedules, so we aim to make routine visits quick and convenient, which our patients appreciate.
