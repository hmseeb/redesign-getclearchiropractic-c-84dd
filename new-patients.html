What to expect

How your care begins

No stone unturned — and no surprises. Here’s the process from first phone call to ongoing care.

Step 01

Complimentary consultation

In the office or via phone and Zoom. Ask anything. Even if you’re unsure what chiropractors do or where you should turn, we’ll get you on the right track — and if chiropractic isn’t right for you, we’ll tell you who we think can help.

Step 02

Visit one: listen & assess

A thorough interview and exam, INSiGHT neurological scans and — for adults, older children and pregnant women — a digital posture assessment, so we can see where your nervous system is holding stress.

Step 03

Visit two: your plan

We walk you through what we found in plain language, set goals together that you actually want to reach, and recommend the techniques best suited to you — including gentle options if you’d rather skip popping and cracking.

Step 04

Ongoing visits

Thanks to that comprehensive initial assessment, typical adjustments are focused and efficient, usually lasting around 5–10 minutes — quick and convenient for busy families.

Fees & payment

Straight answers about cost

Drop in

Up to $75 per session

A drop-in chiropractic session costs up to $75. You’re welcome to pay per visit — there’s no obligation to start a plan.

Care plan

$250 – $450 per month

Most patients choose a customized care plan with affordable monthly payments. The plan’s length and cost depend on your personal goals, challenges and assessments.

Families

Family plans & discounts

We offer family plans and additional discounts for family members, because care works best when the whole household is on board.
